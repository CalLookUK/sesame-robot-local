# Capture

Land raw input as one well-formed note. Infer, write, then report what you chose.

## Steps

1. **Read `CLAUDE.md`** for schema and templates.
2. **Classify.**
   - Finished or in-progress model/unit -> `project` in `Library/Projects/`
   - A recipe I devised or adapted -> `recipe` (set `sources` to the original if adapted)
   - A tip/result about an existing technique or paint -> append under `## My notes`
     of that note rather than making a new one
   - Fragment with no clear subject -> `fleeting` in `Inbox/`
3. **Link.** Projects link the recipes, techniques and paints they used. Add to the
   relevant hub.
4. **Preserve my words.** Keep bullets verbatim; add structure around them.
5. **Report** path, type, and links made.

## Don't

- Don't blend my opinions into source-derived sections.
- Don't invent paint names or ratios I didn't give.
